Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

1. Information Collected

When you use the AuraStudio application, basic device information, anonymous in-app usage statistics, and photos you voluntarily upload when requesting a transformation may be processed to provide the service.

2. AI Photo Processing & Explicit Consent

Before starting each Aura or Package transformation, a clear notice is displayed and your explicit permission is requested; without your consent, no photo is transmitted for AI processing. Upon your approval, uploaded photos are processed by artificial intelligence services solely to generate your selected transformation and are not permanently stored on our servers after completion.

3. Use of Information

Processed data is used to fulfill your requested AI visual transformations, resolve technical issues, and improve service quality. Uploaded photos are never used for profiling, advertising, or marketing purposes.

4. AI Services & Third-Party Sharing

We never sell or transfer your personal data or photos to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Uploaded photos are temporarily transmitted over encrypted connections to artificial intelligence services and essential cloud infrastructure providers strictly to generate your requested visual transformation. These service providers are bound by data privacy and security standards.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures, including encrypted transmission, to safeguard your data and processed visuals. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet can be 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information or photos from children under 13. If discovered, such data will be deleted immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict processing of your personal information, or decline consent. You may decline processing at any time via the permission dialog shown before each transformation, and contact us for any privacy inquiries.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. In the event of changes, the updated policy will be published within the application.
